WARDEN DAY 10
=============

New README. Written to be read, not skimmed. No em dashes, sources credited
where they came from, and a headline you could say out loud.


1. Copy WARDEN-DAY10-FILES out of the zip onto your Desktop.
2. Open it. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
3. Open WARDEN-SEPT15. Ctrl+V.
4. Folders -> Yes / Merge.  Files -> Replace the files in the destination.
5. Delete WARDEN-DAY10-FILES from your Desktop.
6. Double-click START WARDEN.bat, press 2.  You want: 110 passed, 0 failed
7. Double-click PUSH TO GITHUB.bat.
8. Open the repo front page and read it top to bottom.
