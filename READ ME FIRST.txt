WARDEN DAY 11
=============

Warden now reacts when a server announces its tools changed, and no longer
crashes when a pipe closes at the wrong moment. Tested against three real
third-party servers.


1. Copy WARDEN-DAY11-FILES out of the zip onto your Desktop.
2. Open it. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
3. Open WARDEN-SEPT15. Ctrl+V.
4. Folders -> Yes / Merge.  Files -> Replace the files in the destination.
5. Delete WARDEN-DAY11-FILES from your Desktop.
6. Double-click START WARDEN.bat, press 2.  You want: 129 passed, 0 failed
7. Double-click PUSH TO GITHUB.bat.
