WARDEN DAY 8
============

The report page now looks like Warden - navy, brass, the sentinel mark in the
header. Plus a fix: a refusal can no longer be labelled "all clear".


1. Copy WARDEN-DAY8-FILES out of the zip onto your Desktop.
2. Open it. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
3. Open WARDEN-SEPT15. Ctrl+V.
4. Folders -> Yes / Merge.  Files -> Replace the files in the destination.
5. Delete WARDEN-DAY8-FILES from your Desktop.
6. Double-click START WARDEN.bat, press 2.  You want: 110 passed, 0 failed
7. Press 7 to see the new report.
8. Double-click PUSH TO GITHUB.bat to save.
