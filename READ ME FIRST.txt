WARDEN DAY 5
============

These files go INTO your existing WARDEN-SEPT15 folder. Do not keep them as
a separate folder - that creates a separate git repo and the push fails.


1. Copy the WARDEN-DAY5-FILES folder out of the zip onto your Desktop.
2. Open it. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
3. Open WARDEN-SEPT15. Press Ctrl+V.
4. Choose "Replace the files in the destination".
5. In WARDEN-SEPT15, double-click START WARDEN.bat and press 2.
   The last line should end in "0 failed".
6. Double-click PUSH TO GITHUB.bat to save it.
7. Delete WARDEN-DAY5-FILES from your Desktop.


NEW THIS TIME
-------------

Menu option 10 - Protect. Puts Warden in front of an MCP server you already
have in Claude Desktop. Option 11 puts it back exactly as it was.

Do not use option 10 until you have done steps 1 to 6 above.
