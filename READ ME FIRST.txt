WARDEN DAY 7
============

New: menu option 13 sets up a REAL refusal you can screenshot. Option 14
puts everything back.


1. Copy WARDEN-DAY7-FILES out of the zip onto your Desktop.
2. Open it. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
3. Open WARDEN-SEPT15. Ctrl+V.
4. Folders -> Yes / Merge.  Files -> Replace the files in the destination.
5. Delete WARDEN-DAY7-FILES from your Desktop.
6. Double-click START WARDEN.bat, press 2.  You want: 105 passed, 0 failed
7. Press 13. It does everything for you and tells you what to do next.
8. Double-click PUSH TO GITHUB.bat to save.
