WARDEN DAY 12
=============

HTTP transport. Warden can now sit in front of a server reached over the
network, using the same enforcement as the local one.


1. Copy WARDEN-DAY12-FILES out of the zip onto your Desktop.
2. Open it. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
3. Open WARDEN-SEPT15. Ctrl+V.
4. Folders -> Yes / Merge.  Files -> Replace the files in the destination.
5. Delete WARDEN-DAY12-FILES from your Desktop.
6. Double-click START WARDEN.bat, press 2.  You want: 154 passed, 0 failed
7. Double-click PUSH TO GITHUB.bat.
